   While the lemma proves that a consistent terminal curve leads to a consistent model (as
defined by Definition 2.1), it is unclear if the model is well-defined. The reason is that ψt is not

For specific classes of (Xt)t≥t0 and ψ, it is possible to show the existence of a consistent,
positive ψ across the entire interval (see Section 2.3 and Section 2.4).
