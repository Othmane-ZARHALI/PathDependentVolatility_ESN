2.4. Consistency under Polynomial Processes
   The computations of Section 2.3 showed that a reasonable ansatz for ψt is to consider a
function with a quadratic component in x, due to the interpretation of X2t  as the average

to a more general class of processes for (Xt)t≥t0. We introduce polynomial processes and show
how to derive a consistent terminal curve to complete the VDV model. The theoretical insights
of this section are key to approximating a consistent terminal curve for general VIX dynamics
