The mean-reverting process (Xt)t≥t0 has parameters k, defining the mean-reversion speed,
and a long-term mean of Xt defined by θt. Since θt is a time-dependent function, we use it to
calibrate to the VIX future curve (see Appendix A). The diffusion function σLV (t, k) is the local
volatility function of the VIX process, derived following the derivation of Dupire’s formula [21].
Since (Xt)t≥t0 is not a discounted martingale, but has a mean-reversion component for the drift,
we require an adjustment of Dupire’s formula. The derivation of the formula is outlined in [11].
