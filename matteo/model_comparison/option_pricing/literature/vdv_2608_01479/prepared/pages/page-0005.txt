2.2. From VIX to Instantaneous Variance - Coupling Function
   The essence of the joint model defined in eqs. (2.1) to (2.3)  is the coupling function
ψ  : [t0, ∞) × R+ →R+, that links the two stochastic processes through the process of the
instantaneous variance (vt)t≥t0. Although not strictly required, we will assume ψt(x) to be
smooth in the space components (i.e., x 7→ψt(x) ∈C∞, ∀t ≥t0), and at least continuous in
the time direction. The continuity of ψt ensures that (vt)t≥t0 is adapted and almost surely con-
tinuous. A direct consequence of such a coupling model is that vt is a Xt-measurable random
variable for all t ≥t0, since ψ is a deterministic function. Therefore, we have the interpretation
of ψt as the conditional expectation of vt given the current level Xt.

                       E [vt | Xt] = vt = ψt(Xt).                                (2.5)

Here, E [vt | Xt] represents the conditional expectation of vt given the σ-algebra σ(Xt) generated
by Xt.

choice of ψt is not free, as the definition of the VIX through Equation (1.2) must be fulfilled for
all times t ≥t0. The additional condition on the interaction of (Xt)t≥t0 and (St)t≥t0 further
restricts the choice of ψt. We introduce the concept of consistency of the coupling function ψ.
